PRACTICAL 3 — DATA PROTECTION REGULATION COMPLIANCE PLAN FOR OPENAI
1. Aim
To examine the data-protection requirements applicable to an AI service such as ChatGPT and develop a concise compliance framework based on OpenAI's stated privacy practices.

2. Key Compliance Areas
Area	Requirement	OpenAI Policy Indication	Status
Data Collection	Collect data for specified purposes	Data categories and sources are described	✓
Purpose Limitation	Use data for defined purposes	Multiple processing purposes are stated	✓
Data Minimization	Avoid unnecessary personal data	User controls and data practices are provided	◐
Transparency	Inform users about processing	Detailed Privacy Policy provided	✓
User Rights	Access, deletion, correction, portability etc.	Rights and privacy-request mechanisms provided	✓
Security	Protect data against unauthorized access/loss	Technical, administrative and organizational measures stated	✓
Retention	Retain data only as necessary	Retention and deletion rules described	✓
Third Parties	Control processors/service providers	Vendor and service-provider processing described	✓
International Transfers	Apply appropriate transfer safeguards	Policy addresses international processing and legal transfer mechanisms	✓
Children's Data	Provide additional protection	Services not directed to children under 13	✓
Breach Management	Detect, respond to and manage incidents	Security and safety processes are described	◐
Accountability	Maintain evidence of compliance	Privacy requests and erasure records supported	✓

✓ = Addressed in policy | ◐ = Requires ongoing operational assessment

5. Conclusion
OpenAI's Privacy Policy addresses major data-protection areas including data collection, transparency, user rights, security, retention, third-party processing, international transfers, and children's privacy.

For continued compliance, an organization should maintain regular audits, privacy impact assessments, data minimization, security controls, processor reviews, and documented accountability mechanisms.
